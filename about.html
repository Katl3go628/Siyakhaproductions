Who we are

Siyakha Productions is a full-service production company. Our work covers two areas that events and brands depend on: what your audience hears and sees on stage, and what they see on banners, apparel and printed material.

We are not just a print shop or just an audio studio. Both divisions sit within the same company, so you can brief one team for the whole job.
